# Illustration

Xero illustrations are flat vector scenes and objects (coins, invoices, calendars, bells, clocks, growth loops) built around flowing blue ribbons. They do the "human warmth" or "concept" job when a photo is not used.

## Rules

**IL1 MUST: Flat vector, solid fills, no outlines, one darker solid offset shadow per object; white only for highlights and symbols.**
- How to apply: each object has its base colour plus one darker tone of that colour as a hard-edged offset shadow (bottom-right). No soft blurs, no gradients, no line work. White is reserved for highlights, sparkles, motion lines and symbols ($, %, ticks).
- Generation: append verbatim "Xero flat vector illustration style, solid fills, no outlines, one darker offset shadow per object, palette blue green yellow pink purple white, no text, no letters, no logos". Never ask for words in the image.
- Reviewer check: outlines, soft shadows, 3D rendering or photoreal texture in an illustration are failures.

**IL2 SHOULD: Palette blue, green, yellow, pink, purple + white, light from top-left (observed); never recolour a kit illustration.**
- Light source top-left: highlights on the top-left of objects, shadows bottom-right (observed across pages).
- Structure (observed): a central flowing ribbon or swoosh anchors the composition with icons orbiting along it; keep a small white gap between overlapping shapes for legibility.
- Kit illustrations are fixed assets: place them whole with `fit: contain`; do not recolour or crop away the subject.

## How to use in an ad
- Illustration works as `hero` (substitute for a competitor's lifestyle photo or product shot) on a flat field or template white area.
- Pair with at most one bleeding bloom behind it; do not mask an illustration into a bloom (masking is for photos).
- Use the ladder: a kit illustration that does the same job (substitute) beats generating a new one.
