# Charms

Charms are small, soft, rounded 3D-rendered icons (check, thumbs up, sparkle, coin, bell, padlock, growth bars, paper plane...). In ads they are accents that add delight and point at the message.

## Rules

**CH1 MUST: Charms are small accents (1-3, about 8-17% of canvas width) near the hero, in their kit colourways; never the hero.**
- How to apply: place 1-3 charms scattered near or overlapping the hero or headline (e.g. a check next to a claim, a coin beside an invoice). Size each about 0.083-0.167 of canvas width. Set `colour` to one of the asset's `colours_available`.
- Density guide (from testing): minimal = 0-1 charm; balanced = 1-2; loud = 2-3.
- Never use a charm as the hero, never make one large, never recolour by tinting.
- Stacked columns: the brand book shows charms in columns of four inside a rounded purple border with a fixed colour order (blue top, green, yellow/orange, pink; purple border, yellow border = selected). That is a reference/grid layout (observed), not an ad pattern; do not reproduce stacked columns in ads.
- Colour budget: charms add colour; keep the whole ad calm. Prefer charm colours that echo the bloom colours already used.
- Pick by meaning: the charm's subject should relate to the message (check = done, coin = paid, bell = reminder, padlock = secure).
- Why: charms are delight details; oversized or numerous charms make the ad look like a sticker sheet.

## Style (for checking and generation)
- Soft, rounded, 3D-rendered look with consistent lighting and glossy highlights (observed). Some sources show a pixel/faceted edge variant (observed; treat as a variant, not the default).
- Observed colours: blue #0075FF, green #00B85C, yellow/orange #FFB800, pink #FF66CC, purple border #9B51E0 / #6B4CFF.
