# Colour

Xero's palette is navy-led with bright cyan and white, plus the bloom/charm colourways for energy. Everything is flat colour.

## Rules

**CO1 MUST: Use only brand tokens: navy #000856, cyan #00B8E4, sky #59C3E1, white #FFFFFF, template blue #2E5BFF, plus kit colourways.**
| Token | Hex | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| navy | #000856 | Text on light, dark field, CTA fill on light, wordmark |
| cyan | #00B8E4 | Bright field, wordmark on navy, CTA fill on navy |
| sky | #59C3E1 | Lighter bright field, lightblue bloom |
| white | #FFFFFF | Field, cards, text on dark |
| template blue | #2E5BFF (approx., observed) | Only inside background templates |
- Colourways (names, not hex): blooms lightblue, blue, navy, green, yellow, pink, purple; charms blue, green, yellow, pink, purple.
- How to apply: `background_colour` and card/CTA `fill` come from the token list; bloom and charm colour is a colourway name. Never invent a hex (no competitor colours, no off-brand greens or oranges).
- Uncertainty: sources show several "navy" values (#0A1B4D, #0B1B4D, #0B1C48, #0A1E46); use #000856 as the canonical navy. Background blue varies (#1B6BE0 to #2E6BE6 in renders); #2E5BFF is approximate.
- Not for ads: the grey #F2F2F2 / #E6E6E6 and #0B1B4D backgrounds are guideline-slide chrome, not ad colours.

**CO2 MUST: Flat colour everywhere (no gradients); logo and text contrast meets WCAG AAA.**
- No gradients, glows, drop shadows or textures on fields, blooms, cards or type. (Illustrations' offset shadows and charms' 3D rendering are part of those assets, not added effects.)
- Contrast: navy on cyan 9.95:1, navy on white 17.96:1. Navy text on template blue or saturated blooms fails; use white there.
- Palette moves for a `palette` variant: white-led (template + navy text) vs navy-led (navy field + cyan/blue bloom + white text) vs cyan-led (cyan field + navy text + navy bloom). Change the background asset, not just the hex.
- Colour budget: background field + max 3 bloom colours + charm accents. If it feels like a rainbow, cut.

## Other observed colours (context only)
- Product UI: #13B5EA / #0B5CFF header blue, #F5F5F5 background, #00A65A positive, #DC2626 negative (only inside UI assets).
- Illustration palette: see illustration topic.

## Catalog assets
- bloom: 12 shapes x 7 colourways; charm: 65 in 5 colourways; logo: white, navy, cyan variants; templates: fixed blue + white.
