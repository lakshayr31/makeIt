# Bloom

Blooms are Xero's signature organic shapes: smooth, continuous, rounded curves with no sharp corners or geometric edges. They carry energy and movement into an ad. They are a graphic layer, not an illustration or icon.

## Rules

**BL1 MUST: Scale blooms beyond the frame so each bleeds off at least one canvas edge; never show a fully enclosed bloom.**
- How to apply: size the bloom larger than the space it occupies and let its box overshoot the canvas (box x or y below 0, or x+w / y+h above 1). A bloom that floats wholly inside the canvas reads as a sticker or clip-art.
- Edge cases: a bloom used as a photo mask (see photo PH1) may sit mostly inside the canvas, but at least one lobe should still touch or cross an edge, or a second bloom carries the bleed. A bloom "breaking the frame" of a framed photo (PH2) bleeds off the photo frame and usually the canvas too.
- Why: the brand book says blooms "work beautifully when they're cropped"; cropping creates movement and energy.

**BL2 MUST: Blooms are solid, flat, single-colour fills: no gradients, strokes, outlines, shadows or textures; never distort the shape.**
- How to apply: one colourway per bloom instance. Scale uniformly; rotate or mirror if needed, but do not squash, skew or redraw the curve.
- Edge cases: overlapping blooms of different colours are fine (that is layering, BL4); a drop shadow under a bloom is not.
- Reviewer check: any visible gradient, outline or glow on a bloom is a blocking failure.

**BL3 MUST: Max 3 bloom colours per ad, taken only from the bloom colourways.**
- How to apply: count distinct bloom colourways across the whole ad (not counting the background field). Navy used as a bloom counts.
- Why: more than three colours turns the composition noisy and off-brand.

**BL4 SHOULD: Layer blooms (overlap, mixed scale) to add colour and depth.**
- How to apply: one large dominant bloom plus, optionally, a smaller contrasting one overlapping it or crossing behind the hero. Prefer fewer, bigger shapes over many small ones.
- Density guide (from testing): minimal = 1 bloom; balanced = 1-2; loud = 2-3.

**BL5 SHOULD: Go bold top-of-funnel; simpler lower-funnel.**
- Top-of-funnel / awareness / out-of-home: bigger crops, more saturated colour, photography in blooms.
- Lower-funnel / conversion / retargeting: fewer colours (often one), cleaner curves, more white space, product UI as proof.

## Catalog assets
- kind `bloom`, 12 shapes (`bloom-01` .. `bloom-12`), bucket background/shape, each available in 7 colourways: blue, green, lightblue, navy, pink, purple, yellow. Each colourway is a ready PNG; set `colour` to one of these names. Never recolour by tinting.
- The brand book names 12 shapes: Peace, Energy, X, Spark, Grow, Pulse, Harmony, Motion, Balance, Ease, Surge, Stride.
- Aspects range 1.02-1.69 (mostly landscape); pick by the space to fill and rotate if needed.
