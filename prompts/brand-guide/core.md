# Xero brand guide: core rules for making or checking one ad. MUST = blocking, SHOULD = preferred. Detail per topic: call brand_guide(topic).
## bloom
BL1 MUST: Scale blooms beyond the frame so each bleeds off at least one canvas edge; never show a fully enclosed bloom.
BL2 MUST: Blooms are solid, flat, single-colour fills: no gradients, strokes, outlines, shadows or textures; never distort the shape.
BL3 MUST: Max 3 bloom colours per ad, taken only from the bloom colourways.
BL4 SHOULD: Layer blooms (overlap, mixed scale) to add colour and depth rather than scattering small isolated shapes.
BL5 SHOULD: Go bold (big crops, more colour) top-of-funnel; simpler, fewer colours, cleaner curves lower-funnel.
## template
TP1 MUST: Background templates are solid blue + white waves cropped by the canvas edges; add no strokes, gradients, shadows or textures.
TP2 MUST: Pick the template whose format matches the output (1:1 square, 9:16 portrait, 16:9 landscape); never stretch one to fit.
TP3 MUST: Put text on the template's white area (navy text); white text only if it sits fully on the blue area.
TP4 SHOULD: Template blue is approx. #2E5BFF (observed; renders vary) and is NOT navy #000856; treat it as a saturated surface.
## pattern
PT1 MUST: Use bloom patterning only with clear intent (it supports the message); never as decorative filler.
PT2 MUST: A pattern uses at most 3 colours on a navy base, blooms cohesive in form and orientation; no text directly on it.
## photo
PH1 MUST: Top-of-funnel/campaign photos are cropped into a bloom mask; the subject may subtly break the mask for depth.
PH2 SHOULD: Alternative: a rounded-corner framed photo with a bloom breaking out behind or across the frame edge.
## illustration
IL1 MUST: Flat vector, solid fills, no outlines, one darker solid offset shadow per object; white only for highlights and symbols.
IL2 SHOULD: Palette blue, green, yellow, pink, purple + white, light from top-left (observed); never recolour a kit illustration.
## product_ui
PU1 MUST: Show real Xero UI from the kit (white cards on light grey, blue accents); never invent, recolour or crop through key numbers.
## charms
CH1 MUST: Charms are small accents (1-3, about 8-17% of canvas width) near the hero, in their kit colourways; never the hero.
## logo
LG1 MUST: Every ad carries the lowercase xero wordmark asset with clear space; never redraw, recolour, stretch or add effects.
LG2 MUST: Pair wordmark and surface: navy on white or cyan; white or cyan on navy or blue.
LG3 MUST: On a photo or busy bloom area, put the wordmark in a fully rounded pill; no pill on a plain flat surface.
LG4 MUST: Co-brand: Xero left, partner right, "+" between, partner never larger than the Xero wordmark.
## colour
CO1 MUST: Use only brand tokens: navy #000856, cyan #00B8E4, sky #59C3E1, white #FFFFFF, template blue #2E5BFF, plus kit colourways.
CO2 MUST: Flat colour everywhere (no gradients); logo and text contrast meets WCAG AAA (navy/cyan 9.95:1, navy/white 17.96:1).
## type
TY1 MUST: Text colour follows the topmost surface behind it: navy on white/light, white on navy/blue/saturated bloom; no other text colours.
TY2 MUST: Headline is bold condensed sans in sentence case; support copy medium weight; text left-aligned.
TY3 MUST: CTA is a fully rounded pill: navy fill + white text on light surfaces, cyan fill + navy text on navy; at most 22 chars, verb first.
TY4 MUST: Text over a photo, pattern or a bloom edge sits on a white card or flat field, never straddling two surfaces.
## copy
CP1 MUST: Plain, confident, human voice; sentence case; no exclamation marks, superlatives ("best") or competitor wording.
CP2 MUST: Talk about Xero's product benefit for small business; never the competitor's product or category.
## layout
LY1 MUST: Meta safe zones: nothing essential (text, CTA, logo) in the top or bottom 1/12 (8.3%) of the canvas.
LY2 SHOULD: 9:16 Stories/Reels: keep text, CTA and logo clear of the top ~14% and bottom ~21% UI overlays (observed); keep generous white space.
